╔════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╗
║                            KEY IMPROVEMENTS                                    ║
╚════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════╝

✅ CONSISTENT TEXT
   The button text "Continue with Google" never changes, providing a stable
   visual anchor for the user.

✅ CLEAR VISUAL FEEDBACK
   The spinner replaces the Google icon, clearly indicating that authentication
   is in progress without changing the button's primary message.

✅ PREVENTS MULTIPLE CLICKS
   The button is disabled during loading, preventing accidental multiple
   authentication attempts.

✅ BETTER UX
   Users understand that they clicked the correct button and authentication
   is processing, rather than being confused by a text change.

✅ CONSISTENT WITH DESIGN SYSTEM
   Follows Material Design 3 principles for button loading states.
