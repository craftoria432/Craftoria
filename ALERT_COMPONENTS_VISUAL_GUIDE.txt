TIPS & BEST PRACTICES
================================================================================

✓ DO:
  • Keep messages concise and clear
  • Use action-oriented language
  • Provide dismiss option for non-critical alerts
  • Use consistent spacing between alerts
  • Test on different screen sizes

✗ DON'T:
  • Make messages too long (break into multiple)
  • Use CAPS LOCK or excessive punctuation
  • Show too many alerts at once
  • Force users to interact with non-critical alerts
  • Change colors or styling
