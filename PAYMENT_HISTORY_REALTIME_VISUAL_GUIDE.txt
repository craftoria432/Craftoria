    ├─ Order completed
    ├─ Payment created in Firestore
    ├─ ❌ Payment history shows empty
    ├─ ❌ User must manually refresh
    ├─ ❌ Poor user experience
    └─ ❌ Unprofessional

    AFTER (Real-Time Listener):
    ├─ Order completed
    ├─ Payment created in Firestore
    ├─ ✅ Listener notified automatically
    ├─ ✅ Payment history updates instantly (< 1 second)
    ├─ ✅ No manual refresh needed
    ├─ ✅ Excellent user experience
    └─ ✅ Professional, responsive app
