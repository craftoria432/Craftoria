═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Firebase Firestore Update
         │
         ▼
CartRepository.addSnapshotListener() detects change
         │
         ▼
Emits updated cart items via Flow
         │
         ▼
CartViewModel.initializeCart() collects new items
         │
         ▼
_cartItems.value = updatedItems
         │
         ▼
CartScreen recomposes with new data
         │
         ▼
UI displays:
  ✅ Price: PKR 800 (updated)
  ✅ Status: "Negotiated" badge (updated)
  ✅ Subtotal: PKR 800 (recalculated)
  ✅ Total: PKR 950 (recalculated)
