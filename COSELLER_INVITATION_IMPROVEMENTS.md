# Co-Seller Invitation System - Recommended Improvements

## Current Limitations

### 1. No Built-in Seller Discovery
**Problem**: Sellers must know other sellers' email addresses to invite them
**Impact**: Difficult to find and collaborate with new sellers
**Solution**: Implement seller search and directory features

### 2. No Quick Invite from Chat
**Problem**: Sellers chat with potential co-sellers but must manually copy email
**Impact**: Friction in the invitation process
**Solution**: Add "Invite to Store" button in chat
