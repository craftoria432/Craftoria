NAMING DECISION
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

✅ RECOMMENDED: "Seller Application Under Review"

Why:
├─ Professional and clear
├─ Distinguishes from identity verification (ML Kit face detection)
├─ Better for notifications: "Your seller application is under review"
├─ Users understand they're applying to become a seller
└─ Aligns with e-commerce terminology

❌ NOT RECOMMENDED: "Seller Verification Under Review"
   └─ Confuses with identity verification process
